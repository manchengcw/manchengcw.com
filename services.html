Core Business

Each line stands on its own; together they cover the full journey of a collectible or cultural and creative product — from the first sketch to the customer's doorstep.

Trendy Toys & Figurines

Wholesale and retail of trendy toys and figurines. We buy seasonal drops, blind-box series and collector editions, then run them through our own distribution network to retailers, gift shops and online channels.

Wholesale · Retail

Cultural & Creative Gifts

Wholesale and retail of cultural and creative gifts — festival collections, museum partnerships, corporate gifting programmes and licensed merchandise, all developed around a clear cultural story.

Gifting · Licensing

Home Ornaments

Wholesale and retail of home ornaments and decorative objects. Figurines, desk companions and shelf pieces chosen to travel well, price competitively and sit naturally in modern interiors.

Decor · Collecting

Business information consultation for brands and retailers: category sizing, channel mapping, price benchmarking, seasonal calendars and the market reports behind them — delivered as a subscription in the Management App.

How We Work

Six steps from idea to shelf

Brief & Insight

We start with your target shelf, audience and price band, backed by our business information consultation data.

Design & Sample

Our cultural and creative design team develops concepts and prototypes, sharing progress in the Management App.

Source & Inspect
