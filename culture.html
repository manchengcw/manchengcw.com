What Drives Us

Mission, vision and the values in between

01 / MISSION

Make good design travelable

To bring trendy toys, figurines, cultural and creative gifts and home ornaments from the people who make them to the people who love them — with honest quality, fair partnership and a supply chain that never loses sight of the shelf.

02 / VISION

The creative trade house Hong Kong can trust

To become the reference partner for cultural and creative trade in the region — a house where design capability, import and export execution, business information consultation and general trade distribution all live under one roof, and under one app.

03 / VALUES

Four promises we keep

  • Curiosity first — we look before we buy.
  • Small-batch precision — quality over volume.
  • Plain dealing — clear pricing, no surprises.
  • Build it once, well — the app, the process, the standard.
Milestones

How MiniCity grew

Every step below was funded by a real customer order — and every one of them made the next one easier.

2019
2019 · Foundation

A two-person studio in Kwai Chung

MiniCity (Hongkong) Co., Limited begins with a small team buying trendy toys and figurines directly from makers, serving local gift and lifestyle retailers.

2021
2021 · Product

Our first in-house design collection

The cultural and creative product design studio is formalised, producing our first original figurine line and our first cultural and creative gift programme.

2022
2022 · Trade

Import and export desk goes live

We build our own documentation, customs and freight workflow, moving from a buyer to a full trading house across our partner lanes.

2023
2023 · Insight

Business information consultation launched

Our analysts begin publishing category reports on collectible toys, cultural and creative gifts and home ornaments for partners who need more than a price list.

2024
2024 · Platform

MiniCity Management App ships to both stores

The Management App is published on Google Play and the Apple App Store, integrating orders, inventory, supply chain and consultation reporting into one mobile workflow.

2026
2026 · Scale

Eight business lines, one operating system

Wholesale and retail of trendy toys, figurines, cultural and creative gifts and home ornaments sits alongside design, consultation, import and export, and general trade — all steered from the same platform, from the same office in Kwai Chung.

Crew Spirit

Serious about the business.
Not that serious about ourselves.

The MiniCity crew is small on purpose. Everyone here has a mascot, a lane and a habit of asking uncomfortable questions about a product before we commit to it. The mascots are on the website, on the app icon and on the cartons — because a little personality keeps a 40-hour buying week honest.

Compliance & CSR

Trade carefully, document properly

Our business scope is general trade — explicitly excluding regulated businesses such as finance, insurance and lending — and we keep it that way. Import and export of goods and technology is conducted in line with applicable customs, sanctions and export control requirements, and every consumer-facing relationship is governed by published, plain-language policies.

Privacy by default

Our website and the MiniCity Management App are covered by a single privacy policy built around consent, regional rights and transparent advertising practices.

Responsible trade

We screen suppliers and partners against applicable sanctions and restricted-party lists, and we do not trade in regulated categories such as finance, insurance or lending.
